**[ICML 2025]** Our paper “[C-3PO: Compact Plug-and-Play Proxy Optimization to Achieve Human-like Retrieval-Augmented Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.06205)” has been accepted to ICML 2025. It uses three lightweight agents and tree-structured rollout credit assignment to coordinate retrieval decisions, query generation, and document selection without modifying the retriever or language model. :sparkles:
