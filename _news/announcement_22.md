**[Findings of ACL 2026]** Our work on adaptive prompt optimization, “[Adaptive Prompt Optimization for Open-Ended Tasks: Uncertainty Preference as a Secondary Signal](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1692/),” has been accepted to Findings of ACL 2026. It uses semantic entropy to estimate task uncertainty and steer prompt selection toward higher-entropy candidates for creative tasks and lower-entropy candidates for conservative ones. :sparkles:
