**[ICML 2026 Spotlight]** We are delighted that our paper “[Critique-GRPO: Advancing LLM Reasoning with Natural Language and Numerical Feedback](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.03106)” has been accepted to ICML 2026 as a Spotlight; I am the corresponding author. Critique-GRPO integrates natural-language critiques with numerical rewards in online reinforcement learning, allowing a model to learn from both initial responses and critique-guided refinements. :sparkles:
