**[ICML 2026]** Our work “[REFLECTOR: Internalizing Step-wise Reflection against Indirect Jailbreak](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20654)” has been accepted to ICML 2026; I am the corresponding author. REFLECTOR learns structured reflection through teacher-guided fine-tuning, then uses reinforcement learning to internalize autonomous, trajectory-level reflection against multi-step indirect attacks. :sparkles:
