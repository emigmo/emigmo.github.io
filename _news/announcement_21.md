**[ICLR 2026]** We are pleased to share that our benchmark paper “[MCP-SafetyBench: A Benchmark for Safety Evaluation of Large Language Models with Real-World MCP Servers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.15163)” has been accepted to ICLR 2026. Built on real MCP servers, the benchmark supports realistic multi-turn safety evaluation across attacks from the server, host, and user sides, including cross-server workflows. :sparkles:
