**[NeurIPS 2026]** Our paper “[ReSAM: Representation-Level Safety Margin Alignment for Vision–Language Models](https://openreview.net/forum?id=Q8etLWU90b)” has been accepted to NeurIPS 2026. ReSAM aligns safety behavior in the representation space of vision–language models through a lightweight safety-margin objective, improving robustness to pseudo-benign failures with minimal supervision. :sparkles:
