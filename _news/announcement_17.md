**[Findings of ACL 2024]** Our paper “[Beyond One-Preference-Fits-All Alignment: Multi-Objective Direct Preference Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.03708)” has been accepted to Findings of ACL 2024. It introduces MODPO, an RL-free extension of direct preference optimization that combines multiple alignment objectives with configurable weights. :sparkles:
