**[ICML 2026]** “[How Does Reasoning Flow? Tracing Attention-Induced Information Flow for Targeted RL in LLMs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10646)” has been accepted to ICML 2026. The paper introduces FlowTracer, which models attention as a directed acyclic graph, traces answer-targeted information flow, and uses the resulting token credits to focus reinforcement-learning updates on consequential reasoning steps. :sparkles:
