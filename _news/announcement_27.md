**[NeurIPS 2026]** Our paper “[Subliminal Learning as Trait-Direction Drift: A Mechanism and Targeted Control under SFT Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01091)” has been accepted to NeurIPS 2026. The paper identifies trait-direction drift as a mechanism of hidden-trait transfer during SFT distillation and introduces probe-space corridor regularization to suppress this transfer while preserving task performance. :sparkles:
