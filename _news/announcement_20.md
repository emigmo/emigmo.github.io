**[ICLR 2026]** “[Native Reasoning Models: Training Language Models to Reason on Unverifiable Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.11549)” has been accepted to ICLR 2026; I am the corresponding author. The paper presents Native Reasoning Training, treating reasoning traces as latent variables so language models can learn from standard question-answer pairs without expert-written reasoning demonstrations or external verifiers. :sparkles:
